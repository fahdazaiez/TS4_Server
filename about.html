About TS4 Updater

A small project focused on making software updates straightforward.

What is TS4 Updater?

TS4 Updater is a lightweight Windows client connected to a simple version manifest. The client can read an installed version, contact the update service, compare versions and retrieve an authorized release.

The website is intentionally simple: download the client, browse the release history, and keep the update information in one place.

Our approach

TransparentKeep release information easy to understand.
LightweightA small client rather than a heavy launcher.
ReliableUse checksums and clear version metadata.
